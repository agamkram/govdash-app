GovDash

A citizen map of the U.S. federal hierarchy — how it’s organized, what’s nested under what, to help you understand the job before us.

How to use the map

  1. Pick a layout — Icicle, Tree, Circles, or Sankey. The app remembers which chart you left on (only that — not depth, zoom, ZIP, or theme). Direction arrows flip Icicle/Sankey between top-down and sideways.
  2. Depth slider (Icicle) — each step adds another generation under where you are. Depth 1 is the six doors; 11 is the full remaining tree.
  3. Open a place — tap or click a cell to select it and open the detail pane. Use Enter or drill in the map to go deeper; breadcrumbs and Up go back.
  4. Find — search by name. Z looks up House/Senate by ZIP. $ shows Treasury debt figures. L/D switches light and dark for this session. i opens About.
  5. Six doors — Legislative · Executive · Judicial · Agencies · Chartered · International (in that order). On Sankey the last two are labeled Chart and Int so they fit; tap for the full names.
Phone & iPad: press and hold on the map, then slide to scrub across cells. Lift to open that place. (A short coach appears each session until you dismiss it or scrub once.) Pinch to zoom on Icicle and Sankey; Circles zooms by entering a circle.

Where the data comes from

Structure is built from official sources and refreshed offline into this app — not guessed.

  • GSA Federal Hierarchy Crosswalk — skeleton of orgs and parent links (main map identity).
  • OPM Federal Workforce Data (EHRI Status) — civilian employee counts when we can match a node. Snapshot Jun 2026. Civilian only — not military end strength, USPS, or contractors. Legislative / most Judicial orgs often have no OPM row.
  • U.S. Government Manual — mission and leadership when available.
  • SAM.gov — status overlays where matched.
  • Treasury — public debt figures on the $ page.

If employees are blank, that usually means OPM doesn’t publish a count for that org in this file — not that we hid one.

Other doors on the main map

Chartered

34 nodes · 33 leaves · ~1,100 civilians where OPM matches. Federally chartered corporations, GSEs, and similar — on the main map after Agencies. Icicle/Circles weight them narrower than Legislative; Sankey labels the door Chart.

International

117 nodes · 116 leaves · almost no OPM coverage. International organizations (IGOs) the U.S. participates in. Leaf-heavy; Icicle/Circles weight them down; Sankey labels the door Int.

Glossary

  • Node — any org in the hierarchy.
  • Leaf — node with no children.
  • Department — kind=department (18 Cabinet-style orgs under Executive).
  • Beneath — how many nodes sit under that department in this map (not just direct children).
  • Workforce shown — civilian employees under the title (direct OPM match or sum of children).
  • Depth — generations under your current focus on the Icicle slider.
  • Chart / Int — Sankey short labels for Chartered and International.

Counts verified against merged product + Beyond doors · not a live OPM query · Jun 2026 workforce snapshot.
