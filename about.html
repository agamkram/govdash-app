GovDash

A citizen map of the U.S. federal hierarchy — how it’s organized, what’s nested under what, and where official data lets us show more.

How to use the map

  1. Pick a layout — Icicle (default), Tree, Circles, or Sankey. Direction arrows flip Icicle/Sankey between top-down and sideways.
  2. Depth slider (Icicle) — each step adds another generation under where you are. Depth 1 is the four doors; 11 is the full remaining tree.
  3. Open a place — tap or click a cell to select it and open the detail pane. Use Enter or drill in the map to go deeper; breadcrumbs and Up go back.
  4. Find — search by name. Z looks up House/Senate by ZIP. $ shows Treasury debt figures. ∞ opens Beyond (chartered & international).
Phone & iPad: press and hold on the map, then slide to scrub across cells. Lift to open that place. (A short coach appears the first time.) Pinch to zoom on Icicle and Sankey; Circles zooms by entering a circle.

Where the data comes from

Structure is built from official sources and refreshed offline into this app — not guessed.

  • GSA Federal Hierarchy Crosswalk — skeleton of orgs and parent links (main map identity).
  • OPM Federal Workforce Data (EHRI Status) — civilian employee counts when we can match a node. Snapshot Jun 2026. Civilian only — not military end strength, USPS, or contractors. Legislative / most Judicial orgs often have no OPM row.
  • U.S. Government Manual — mission and leadership when available.
  • SAM.gov — status overlays where matched.
  • Treasury — public debt figures on the $ page.

If employees are blank, that usually means OPM doesn’t publish a count for that org in this file — not that we hid one.

Glossary

  • Node — any org in the hierarchy.
  • Leaf — node with no children.
  • Department — kind=department (18 Cabinet-style orgs under Executive).
  • Workforce shown — civilian employees under the title (direct OPM match or sum of children).
  • Depth — generations under your current focus on the Icicle slider.
